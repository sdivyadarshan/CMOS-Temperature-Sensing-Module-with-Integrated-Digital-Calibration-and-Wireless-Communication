# Hardware Components

| Component | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| ESP32 DevKit | 1 | Main controller, data processing and Wi-Fi |
| DS18B20 waterproof digital temperature sensor | 1 | Temperature measurement |
| 4.7 kΩ resistor | 1 | One-Wire data-line pull-up |
| Breadboard | 1 | Temporary circuit assembly |
| Male-to-male jumper wires | Approximately 10 | Breadboard connections |
| Male-to-female jumper wires | Approximately 10 | Sensor-to-board connections |
| USB cable | 1 | ESP32 power and programming |

## Notes

- The ESP32 is powered through its USB input during the demonstrated prototype.
- The DS18B20 is a digital temperature sensor with a One-Wire interface.
- The 4.7 kΩ pull-up resistor connects the DATA line to 3.3 V.
- Jumper-wire quantities are approximate and depend on the physical layout.
- No separate battery is included in this prototype.
